# 📜 Code of Conduct — marko1olo/dental-crm

> **Community Standards, Technical Integrity & Engineering Meritocracy**  
> Maintained by the **Жирняк & Адольф Петушков** Engineering Syndicate

---

## 🎯 1. Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers of **marko1olo/dental-crm** are committed to making participation in our project a constructive, technically rigorous, and honest experience for everyone. We welcome contributions regardless of background, provided they meet our engineering and domain standards.

---

## 🛡️ 2. Core Community Standards

We hold all participants to high professional and technical principles:

### 2.1 Technical Authenticity & Truthfulness
* **Radical Honesty**: Be direct, factual, and objective. Never disguise incomplete solutions, untested logic, or regressions.
* **Reject AI Slop & Sycophancy**: Do not submit auto-generated boilerplate, shallow patches, or superficial pull requests to inflate metrics. Every line of code must be understood, verified, and defended by its author.
* **Constructive Critique**: Reviews focus strictly on code quality, mathematical correctness, performance, and security. We critique code, not people.

### 2.2 Professional Interaction
* **Respect Maintainer Time**: Before opening an issue or PR, search existing discussions, verify reproducibility, and provide clean evidence.
* **Warm Collaboration**: We value thoughtful, experienced, and dedicated open-source collaboration. When engaging with external contributors, communicate clearly and professionally.
* **Zero Hostility**: Harassment, derogatory comments, personal attacks, and trolling are strictly prohibited.

---

## ⚖️ 3. Enforcement & Responsibilities

The project maintainers (**Жирняк & Адольф Петушков**) are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards:

1. **Correction**: Maintainers will provide clear, constructive feedback when a contribution fails to meet standards or introduces unnecessary noise.
2. **Rejection**: Pull requests containing unverified code, duplicate spam, or aggressive behavior will be closed without merge.
3. **Exclusion**: Repeated or egregious violations of conduct will result in temporary or permanent bans from the repository.

---

## 📬 4. Reporting & Attribution

If you observe unacceptable behavior or have questions regarding conduct enforcement, contact the repository maintainers directly via repository issue channels or email.
