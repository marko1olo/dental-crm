## 2. LOGIC CHAIN

1. **Step 1 (OID Analysis)**:
   - Observation 1 proves that `NSI_SEMD_DOC_TYPES` in `egiszRemdPresets.ts:29` is `"1.2.643.5.1.13.13.11.1005"`.
   - By statutory Russian NSI registry specification, OID `1.2.643.5.1.13.13.11.1005` represents ICD-10 (МКБ-10), while the registry of medical documentation forms is `1.2.643.5.1.13.13.11.1522`.
   - The canonical definition in `packages/shared/src/cda/oids.ts:40` and validator in `packages/shared/src/cda/validator.ts:806` enforce `1.2.643.5.1.13.13.11.1522`.
   - *Inference*: Any CDA R2 document generated by `cdaR2XmlBuilder.ts` will fail statutory REMD validation against Minzdrav schematron.

2. **Step 2 (Signature Standard Compliance)**:
   - Observation 2 demonstrates that `cdaR2XmlBuilder.ts:692-702` generates an embedded `<ds:Signature>` block using `enveloped-signature`.
   - Minzdrav and FSTEC/FSB regulations under 63-FZ require detached CMS/CAdES-BES PKCS#7 signatures (`document.xml.sig`), strictly forbidding embedded XML-DSig.
   - *Inference*: The frontend web builder actively bypasses security constraints (`disallowEnvelopedSignature: false`) to construct a non-compliant signature format.

3. **Step 3 (Production Readiness of Signing Workflow)**:
   - Observation 3 shows `handleSignMoDocument` in `EgiszRemdHubModal.tsx:570-584` creating a mock signature string (`U0VNRF8xMDVfTU9fU0lHTkFUVVJFCg==` = `SEMD_105_MO_SIGNATURE\n`).
   - *Inference*: The MO signing step in the Web Hub does not invoke CryptoPro CSP and cannot produce legally binding signatures for REMD submission.

4. **Step 4 (Prescription Regulatory & Ergonomic Assessment)**:
   - Observation 4 confirms that `forms107_1u.ts` correctly validates all statutory invariants of Order 1094n (form types, validity limits, item limits, contraindications, and DDI safety).
   - Print generation in `clinicalHtmlRenderers.ts` strictly satisfies typographic standards (PT Astra Serif / Times New Roman, A5 layout, stamp placeholders, offline SVG QR code).
   - Ergonomics satisfy solo doctor and small clinic autonomy via 1-click presets without any cartoon emojis.
